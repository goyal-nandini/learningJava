==============================
FAKE INSURER POLICY RULES
==============================

1. POLICY ELIGIBILITY
---------------------
- Applicant must be between 18 and 70 years of age.
- Valid government ID is mandatory.
- Policyholder must disclose all pre-existing conditions.

2. PREMIUM PAYMENT RULES
------------------------
- Premium must be paid monthly, quarterly, or annually.
- A grace period of 15 days is allowed after due date.
- Policy will lapse if payment is not received within grace period.

3. CLAIM CONDITIONS
-------------------
- Claims must be filed within 30 days of incident.
- Required documents:
  * Claim form
  * Identity proof
  * Medical or incident report
- False claims will result in permanent blacklisting.

4. COVERAGE RULES
-----------------
- Maximum coverage limit: ₹10,00,000
- Pre-existing diseases covered after 2 years waiting period.
- Accidental coverage starts immediately after policy activation.

5. EXCLUSIONS
-------------
- Self-inflicted injuries
- Damage due to illegal activities
- War or nuclear-related incidents
- Cosmetic procedures unless medically necessary

6. POLICY CANCELLATION
----------------------
- Policyholder may cancel within 14 days (free-look period).
- Refund will be issued after deducting administrative charges.
- Insurer may cancel policy in case of fraud or misrepresentation.

7. RENEWAL TERMS
----------------
- Policy must be renewed before expiry date.
- Late renewal allowed within 30 days with penalty.
- Benefits may reset if renewal is delayed beyond allowed period.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
---------------------
- All disputes subject to jurisdiction of local courts.
- Arbitration may be used before legal proceedings.
